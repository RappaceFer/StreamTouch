# StreamTouch 1.0.0

First public release. Play World of Warcraft on Android, streamed from your PC through Sunshine, with touch controls made for it.
Goes with the WoW addon **Streaming Touch 2.0**.

## Highlights

- Move stick and camera stick, with taps and double taps for Use, auto run, target and jump.
- The screen works as a mouse: tap to click, hold to right click, drag, two fingers to scroll.
- On-screen buttons you can move, hide and restyle (shape, color, icon), with a medieval gold-and-leather look.
- Round menu: controller / touch mode, settings, guide, Shift / Ctrl / Alt for the next press, auto run, dive.
- Keyboard: your phone keyboard in your own language, or the Swiss PC keyboard of the app.
- English and French.

## Install

Download `StreamTouch-1.0.0.apk` below, install it, add your PC and pair it with Sunshine.
Requires Android 8.0 or newer (64-bit ARM).
